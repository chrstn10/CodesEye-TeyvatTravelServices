About Inazuma

Traveler's Journal

Field Notes · Inazuma

An island nation east across the sea, wrapped in mist and known for keeping its own customs closely. Inazuma's shrines, torii gates, and seasonal cherry blossoms give the archipelago a quiet, ceremonial character, even in its busiest trading ports.

Featured Stops

Highlights of the Inazuma Route

1

Narukami Island Grand Shrine

Inazuma's largest shrine, reached by a long stone stairway lined with torii gates up Mt. Yougou. Visitors pass beneath the shrine's storied sakura tree before drawing a fortune slip at the top.

2

Ritou Trading Port

A lively checkpoint island connecting the archipelago's inner and outer regions, lined with street food stalls, artisan stands, and a small fishing village.

3

Tenshukaku

Inazuma's iconic keep, an imposing tiered tower that dominates the island skyline. It is best viewed from the harbor at sunset.
