About Liyue

Traveler's Journal

Field Notes · Liyue

A wealthy harbor nation on Teyvat's eastern coast, framed by dramatic stone peaks and old mining country. Liyue built its fortune on trade, including tea, jade, and shipping routes that reach every corner of the continent, and it still marks the turning of each year with the Lantern Rite, when thousands of paper lanterns are released over the harbor at once.

Featured Stops

Highlights of the Liyue Route

1

Liyue Harbor

Teyvat's largest trading harbor, lined with terraced rooftops, lantern-lit streets, and stalls selling everything from tea to carved jade. Time your visit with the annual Lantern Rite to see thousands of lanterns released over the water at once.

2

Mt. Aocang

A dramatic mountain range on the city's outskirts, known for scenic overlooks and local folklore passed down through generations of traders.

3

Wangshu Inn

A multi-tiered inn built atop a stone outcrop in Dihua Marsh, long favored by traveling merchants for its cliffside views stretching toward Mt. Qingce and the Jueyun Karst.
