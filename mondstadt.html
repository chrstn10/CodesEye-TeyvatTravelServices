About Mondstadt

Traveler's Journal

Field Notes · Mondstadt

A city of freedom built on an island at the heart of Cider Lake, in Teyvat's northeast. Mondstadt is known for its open hospitality, its love of music and wine, and the windmills that turn along its outer hills. Locals say the mountain breezes carry the scent of dandelion seeds across the water each spring, a small and welcome reminder that this is a place built for travelers to wander freely.

Featured Stops

Highlights of the Mondstadt Route

1

Cider Lake

A wide, mirror-still lake ringed by windmills and wildflowers, clear enough to see the lakebed below. Its waters are said to give Mondstadt's ciders and wines their crisp, local character.

2

Windrise

A grassy hillside crowned by a single massive tree, long celebrated in local legend as a symbol of the city's heroes. Best visited at golden hour, when the whole hillside turns amber.

3

Cathedral of Mondstadt

Soaring stone arches, stained glass, and a bell tower mark this former noble estate, now a quiet place of reflection open to all visitors.
