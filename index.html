Our Story

From Solo Traveler to Sole Proprietor

Welcome to Teyvat, a land shaped by seven elements and watched over by seven Archons, where every nation carries its own customs, landscape, and story. Teyvat Travel Services designs and guides tours through all seven of them.

Teyvat Travel Services began as a notebook of hand-drawn maps and travel notes, gathered over years of independent journeys across all seven nations. What started as word-of-mouth trip planning for friends grew into a full-time agency, though it is still run single-handedly by its Director, who now juggles every inquiry, itinerary, and booking by hand across Messenger, SMS, and email.

With seven nations to cover, each with its own pricing and seasonal details, the same questions keep coming in one at a time: What are the packages for Liyue? How much for a group of four? Is this date open? This website is the agency's first step toward a single, organized home for its brand, its packages, and the answers clients ask for most.

Meet the Director

A Traveler First, now an Owner

Lumine, Founder & Director

Before founding Teyvat Travel Services, Lumine spent the better part of a decade traveling independently across all seven nations. She slept in countryside inns near Mondstadt, learned trade routes through Liyue Harbor, joined shrine rituals in Inazuma, studied in the academic quarters of Sumeru, watched opera performances in Fontaine, followed festival crowds in Natlan, and ventured into the frontier of Snezhnaya.

"I never wanted to build a travel agency that sells destinations it's never seen. Every route on this site is one I've walked myself. I just happen to also handle the invoices now."

That hands-on, seen-it-herself approach has always been the foundation of how Lumine runs the agency. It is also why this website carries her voice throughout, not as a generic agency page, but with the same personal touch she has built her business on, now available to answer the questions she used to field one message at a time.

Frequently Asked Questions

General Booking Questions

How do I book a tour?

Send us a message through Facebook Messenger, SMS, or email with your preferred nation, travel dates, and group size. We'll reply with availability and next steps, typically within 24 hours.

How much would a package cost for a group of 4?

All package prices are listed per person on each nation's page. Multiply the base price by your group size for an estimate. For example, the Liyue package (₱15,000 per person) would total ₱60,000 for a group of 4. Let us know your group size when you inquire and we will confirm the exact total.

Is my preferred date available?

Availability is confirmed per batch and per nation. Send us your preferred travel dates through any of our contact channels and we will personally confirm whether a slot is open.

What payment methods do you accept?

We currently accept bank transfer, GCash, and cash payment for down payments and full balances. Payment details are shared directly once your itinerary is confirmed.

Can an itinerary be customized?

Minor adjustments (meal preferences, added rest days) can often be accommodated on request. Let us know your needs when you inquire and we'll do our best to work them in.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellation terms depend on how close to the travel date the request is made. Full terms are shared and confirmed with each client at the time of booking.
