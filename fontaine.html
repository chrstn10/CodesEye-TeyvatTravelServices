About Fontaine

Traveler's Journal

Field Notes · Fontaine

A coastal nation built around canals, courts, and mechanical ingenuity. Fontaine's justice system runs as visibly as its waterways, and its opera houses and fashion houses give the capital a distinct sense of theater and craft.

Featured Stops

Highlights of the Fontaine Route

1

Court of Fontaine

The nation's grand seat of justice, crowned by the Palais Mermonia, Fontaine's highest authority. Public proceedings are open to visitors curious about the nation's legal traditions.

2

Opera Epiclese

A celebrated opera house in the Erinnyes district, known for its acoustics and elaborate stage design.

3

Lucine Fountain

An elegant central fountain plaza and the meeting point for much of the city's public life, especially beautiful in the evening.
