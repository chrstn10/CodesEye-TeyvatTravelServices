About Sumeru

Traveler's Journal

Field Notes · Sumeru

A nation split between two very different landscapes: a rainforest thick enough to hide entire settlements in its canopy, and a desert holding the ruins of a much older civilization. At its center sits a renowned academy city, long regarded as the region's seat of learning.

Featured Stops

Highlights of the Sumeru Route

1

Sumeru City Academy

A tiered desert city built around its renowned academy, where domed libraries and open study courts sit beside busy market avenues, long regarded as the region's seat of wisdom.

2

Gandharva Ville

A rainforest settlement built among the treetops and linked by rope bridges and wooden walkways, known locally as the city above the forest.

3

Caravan Ribat

A historic desert waystation that once hosted trade caravans, sheltered beneath the great wall that shields the region from sandstorms.

Common Questions

Sumeru Package FAQs

Why is this tour 3 nights despite being only 3 days?

The itinerary includes an additional rest night between the rainforest and desert legs of the journey due to the travel distance between regions.

Is this suitable for travelers unfamiliar with rainforest terrain?

Yes, all rainforest walkways are guided and built for tourist access; no prior hiking experience is required.

Can this package be booked for just 2 travelers?

Yes, the Sumeru package is priced per person with no minimum group size.
