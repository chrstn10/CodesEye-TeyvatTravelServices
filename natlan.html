About Natlan

Traveler's Journal

Field Notes · Natlan

A nation of volcanic highlands and canyon country in Teyvat's west, where communities gather for competitive festivals and tribal celebrations built around fire and endurance. Its natural hot springs offer a warm counterpoint to the dramatic, ash-toned terrain.

Featured Stops

Highlights of the Natlan Route

1

Tepecac Stadium

A dramatic open-air arena carved into volcanic rock, home to the nation's celebrated competitive festivals and tribal gatherings.

2

Children of the Echoes Canyon

A striking canyon landscape shaped by volcanic activity, offering some of the most dramatic scenery in the tour.

3

People of the Springs Basin

A basin of natural thermal springs where travelers can relax after a day of exploring the surrounding terrain.
